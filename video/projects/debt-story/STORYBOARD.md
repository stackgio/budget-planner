phrase boundaries are measured. Word anchors marked "~" sit between two measured dips and

## Financial accuracy rules applied

- The only dollar figures on screen are the narrated $500 and $1,000, shown as an illustrative savings balance.
- There are no repair costs, bill amounts, debt balances or progress percentages, because none were supplied.
- Scene 9 carries the footnote "Illustrative example. Expense costs vary."
- The account cards are generic illustrations, not bank screenshots, and no transactions are shown.
