# DMM-001 — originality and adaptation notes

**Source format:** REF-0001 (@pathlightvisuals, IG, 8.1M views): "Same $10K. Two moves." A stacked A/B split, beat-synced counters, and a header swap to the moral.

## Borrowed (non-protectable principles)
- Identical input, two choices, stacked split screen.
- Numbers update continuously while time passes, then settle on the result.
- The headline swaps to the takeaway at the end.
- From REF-0004: the losing side loses colour. From REF-0005: money put in vs value as two lines, so the gap is the growth.
