## Instagram / Facebook Reels
Waiting ten years to invest doesn't cost you the small years. It costs you the biggest ones. 🌳

Think of every invested year as a ring in a tree trunk. The outer rings are the widest: in this example, the last 10 years add more than the first 30 combined. Start at 35 instead of 25 and those are the rings that never grow.

Example: $250/month to age 65, 7%/yr average return assumed, compounded monthly. Start at 25 ≈ $656K. Start at 35 ≈ $305K. Returns are not guaranteed. Not financial advice.
