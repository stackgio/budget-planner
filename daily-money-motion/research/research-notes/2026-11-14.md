**Tools used:** vidIQ connector — `instagram_tiktok_outlier_search` (IG + TikTok, posted after 2026-05-01, ≥100K views, ≤40 s, finance concepts), `outliers` (YouTube Shorts, ≥500K views), `watch_shortform_content` (AI scene-by-scene analysis of 5 posts).
**Limits:** View counts are what vidIQ indexed today. Retention, shares, and saves are not public, so they are unknown. Scene analysis is an AI watch, not a human frame-by-frame review. No video files were downloaded. Facebook Reels was not searchable with the available tools.

## Pattern found
In the last 30 days the clearest finance breakout format is the **identical-input A/B comparison**. Same money, two (or three) choices, numbers that move on every beat, and a header that swaps to the moral at the end. Five of the eight IG outliers use it (8.1M, 3.9M, 2.2M, 1.1M, 1.1M views). Most are 10–18 s, use music only, and are built to loop.
